# Code of Conduct

OS Lab is a learning project. Many contributors are students opening their first pull request, so we hold ourselves to a simple standard: make it easy and safe to learn here.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Explain, don't belittle; there are no stupid questions.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and make it specific and actionable.
- Credit others' work and ideas.
- Respect differing experience levels, backgrounds, languages and time zones.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and chat) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer, Daniel Kwadwo Takyi, at danielkwadwotakyi@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which you can read for fuller guidance.
